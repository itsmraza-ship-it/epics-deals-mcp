# Privacy — Epics Deals MCP Tools

_Last updated: [date of publication]_

This page covers the Epics Deals MCP server at `mcp.epicsdeals.com` (the "Tools"). Use of the Epics Deals website and client services is covered by our main privacy policy.

## What the Tools do
The Tools answer questions about US LLC formation costs, EIN and ITIN timelines, and IRS/state filing deadlines. They work entirely from published, public data (state fee schedules and IRS deadlines) combined with Epics Deals' public pricing.

## Data we collect
**None.** Specifically:

- No account or sign-in is required or offered.
- The Tools do not ask for, receive, or store names, email addresses, passport details, tax IDs, or any other personal information. Tool inputs are limited to a US state, an entity type, a formation date, a residency status, and a form type.
- We do not set cookies and do not run analytics or advertising trackers on the MCP endpoint.
- Standard infrastructure logs (timestamps, request counts, error rates) may be kept by our hosting provider for security and reliability; they are not used to identify individuals and are not sold or shared for marketing.

## What happens if you contact us
Links in tool answers lead to epicsdeals.com and our WhatsApp. Anything you share after following those links is handled under our main privacy policy and only with your direction.

## Changes
If the Tools ever begin handling personal data, this page will be updated and the directory listing amended before that happens.
